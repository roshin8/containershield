Welcome to Container Shield

Per-container fingerprint protection for Firefox

1

Open a Container Tab

Container Shield works with Firefox Multi-Account Containers. Each container gets its own unique fingerprint that cannot be linked to other containers.

2

Click the Shield Icon

Click the Container Shield icon in your toolbar to see protection status, adjust settings, and view which fingerprinting techniques websites are using.

3

Choose Your Protection Level

Balanced mode is recommended for most users. It adds noise to fingerprinting APIs without breaking websites. Use Strict for maximum privacy.

4

Browse Privately

That's it! Container Shield now protects 50+ fingerprinting APIs. Each container has a unique, consistent fingerprint that won't change between page loads.
